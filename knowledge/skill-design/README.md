# Skill design

This topic maintains reusable design guidance: what belongs in a skill, how to allocate judgment between models and people, and how to improve instructions through observed behavior.

Start with the [skill design principles](./skill-design-principles.md). They synthesize primary material from Matt Pocock, Anthropic, OpenAI, Superpowers, Vercel, and Hamel Husain / Shreya Shankar, with explicit project choices and boundaries.

## Status and maintenance

2026-10-25: The owner endorsed Matt's direction and authorized research into other authors to establish this project's design philosophy. The principles are adopted authoring guidance; their effects on particular models, hosts and tasks still require verification. Research support is not proof of effectiveness in this project.

Evidence and source-specific claims remain in the [research directory](../../research/harness/skill-repository/README.md). Update this topic for reusable corrections; keep candidates, cases and trial results in their initiatives. This topic does not maintain duplicate package formats, installation mechanisms or runtime instructions.
