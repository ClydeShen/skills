# Human-AI Meta Thinking research

This directory preserves source material, evidence and exploration of how people and AI select, test and change thinking approaches. Current synthesis lives in the [Meta Thinking knowledge topic](../../../knowledge/meta-thinking/README.md).

## Materials

- [Exploratory draft](./meta-ai-thinking-working-draft-2026-10-25.md): the user-supplied PRD-style Working Draft, archived in Markdown and translated into English. It retains background, requirements, hierarchy hypotheses, examples and open questions. The filename date is the archival date.
- [Computation, collaboration and automation](./metareasoning-foundations-2026-10-25.md): primary sources and hypotheses about thinking cost, stopping, proactive intervention and work allocation.
- [Evidence limits for abstraction, criticism and collaboration](./interaction-evidence-and-evaluation-2026-10-25.md): positive and negative experiments, applicability limits and proposed comparisons.
- [Lightweight intervention and intent exploration](./lightweight-intervention-2026-10-25.md): subsequent requirements, preference construction, intervention burden, simple design and implicit activation.
- [Event-triggered memory and profiles](./event-driven-memory-2026-10-25.md): event evidence, lazy updates, attribution limits, temporal revision and selection bias.
